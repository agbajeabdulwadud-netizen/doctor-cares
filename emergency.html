Go to Emergency Room Immediately

  • Chest pain, pressure, or symptoms of heart attack.
  • Sudden weakness, facial numbness, or stroke signs.
  • Acute pediatric respiratory distress or blue lips.
  • Severe trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, or compound fractures.

Schedule Urgent Outpatient Clinic

  • Low-grade fevers, cold, or flu symptoms.
  • Minor strains, mild sprains, and shallow scrapes.
  • Routine medication refills and checkups.
  • Non-urgent oncology or pediatric consultations.
