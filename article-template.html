Early Detection Strategies in Modern Oncology

Clinical Reviewer: Dr. Alao Oko Chicken, Consultant Oncologist • Published: November 2026

Early diagnostic intervention remains the single most impactful factor in achieving positive long-term oncology outcomes.

1. Recognizing Subtle Diagnostic Indicators

Routine screening guidelines are established to detect cellular anomalies prior to the onset of acute physical symptoms. Periodic screenings are strongly advised for individuals with hereditary predispositions.

2. Diagnostic Modalities at Doctor Cares

Our Abeokuta campus utilizes computerized tomography (CT), high-resolution ultrasound, and automated hematology testing lines to accelerate clinical turnaround.
